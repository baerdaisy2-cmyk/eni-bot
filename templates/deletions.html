How the score is calculated

aggression_score = removed ÷ total comments in that subreddit. It counts both your own comments marked gone on the watchlist and any comment the scraper found showing as [deleted] or [removed].

A fast removal is one that vanished inside an hour of being added — that pattern points at an automod rule or a filter rather than a human. Points at. Reddit never says why.
