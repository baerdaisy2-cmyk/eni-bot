How this works

Open a comment link in a new tab and look at it. If the comment is there, click still up. If it's missing or shows as removed, click it's gone.

Two of your comments gone on the same thread blacklists that thread. More than half your comments gone in a subreddit, over at least five comments, flags the whole subreddit. A comment that vanished under an hour is recorded as a suspected mod or filter action — suspected, not proven.
